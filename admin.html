➕ How to Add a Product

1

Tap the ➕ Add tab at the top

2

Tap Select photos — pick up to 6 photos from your phone. Any filename is fine, they rename automatically.

3

Fill in Product Name, Category, Price and Description

4

Type the Image Filename e.g. carpet.10 — this is what the site uses to find the photo

5

Select a Badge (HOT, SALE, NEW or none) and Stock Status

6

Tap 💾 Save Product to Website — photos upload automatically and product goes live in under 1 minute ✅

✏️ How to Edit a Product

1

Tap the ✏️ Edit tab

2

Type the product name in the search box e.g. Fluffy Carpet

3

Tap the product from the results — the form fills automatically with all current details

4

Change what you need — name, price, description, badge or stock status

5

Tap 💾 Save Changes to Website — updates go live immediately ✅

💡 To mark a product as Out of Stock — tap Edit, select ❌ Out of Stock, tap Save. The product will show a red OUT OF STOCK badge on the website.

🗑️ How to Delete a Product

1

Tap the 🗑️ Delete tab

2

Search for the product name

3

Tap the product — a confirmation panel appears showing the product photo, name and price

4

Tap 🗑️ Yes, Delete from Website — product is removed immediately ✅

⚠️ Deleting a product is permanent and cannot be undone. If you just want to hide it temporarily, use Edit → mark as Out of Stock instead.

📸 How to Upload Images

Images are uploaded automatically when you save a product. You can also upload them separately from the Settings tab.

1

Tap the ⚙️ Settings tab

2

Scroll to Upload Product Images

3

Select the category and product number e.g. Carpets + 11 = carpet.11.jpg

4

Tap Select photos — pick photos from your phone (any name is fine)

5

Tap 📤 Upload & Auto-Rename to GitHub — photos appear on website immediately ✅

🔑 GitHub Token Setup

You only need to do this once. The token allows the admin panel to save products and images directly to your website.

1

Go to github.com and log in

2

Tap your profile picture → Settings

3

Scroll down → tap Developer settings

4

Tap Personal access tokens → Tokens (classic)

5

Tap Generate new token (classic)

6

Tick repo → tap Generate token → copy it immediately

7

Tap ⚙️ Settings tab here → paste token → tap Save Token

🔒 Keep your token private — treat it like a password. Never share it with anyone.
