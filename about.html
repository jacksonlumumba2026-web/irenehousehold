✦ How We Started

From a Passion for Beautiful Homes

Irene Household Collections was born from a deep love for creating beautiful, comfortable living spaces. What started as a small venture has grown into one of Nairobi's most trusted household items suppliers.

We believe that every home deserves quality — whether it's a cozy apartment, a family house, a hotel or an Airbnb property. That's why we source only the best products at the most competitive prices in the market.

From luxurious duvets and beddings to stylish furniture, premium kitchenware to beautiful carpets — we have everything your home needs under one roof.
